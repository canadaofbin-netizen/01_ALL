# Kupffer_et_al_2024_Careless_Responding_MFC

This study explores methods for detecting [[Careless_responding]] in [[Multidimensional_forced-choice__MFC__format|multidimensional forced-choice (MFC)]] questionnaires. The authors adapted existing indices from rating scale formats and developed new ones specific to MFC data, such as [[Triplet_variance]] and [[LongOrder_indices]]. Through an online survey and a simulation study, they demonstrated that careless responding exists in MFC formats and can be effectively identified using a combination of indices like response time, rank order repetition, triplet variance, and [[Instructed_response_triplets]]. The research provides practical recommendations for screening and improving data quality in MFC assessments.

See authors: [[Rebekka_Kupffer]], [[Eunike_Wetzel]] (if applicable).

## Information

### Added from [[kupffer-et-al-2024-detecting-careless-responding-in-multidimensional-forced-choice-questionnaires.pdf]] on 2026-08-01
- Careless responding is an important issue that affects data quality in multidimensional forced-choice (MFC) questionnaires, just as it does in rating scales. ([kupffer-et-al-2024-detecting-careless-responding-in-multidimensional-forced-choice-questionnaires.pdf])
- Latent profile analysis indicates that while the majority of participants (76-84%) respond carefully, a distinct subset exhibits different levels and types of careless responding. ([kupffer-et-al-2024-detecting-careless-responding-in-multidimensional-forced-choice-questionnaires.pdf])
- Indices such as triplet variance, LongOrderMax, instructed response triplets, and response time index perform best in detecting careless responding in MFC data. ([kupffer-et-al-2024-detecting-careless-responding-in-multidimensional-forced-choice-questionnaires.pdf])
- The Mahalanobis distance index did not prove to be an effective measure for detecting careless responding in the MFC format. ([kupffer-et-al-2024-detecting-careless-responding-in-multidimensional-forced-choice-questionnaires.pdf])
- Removing participants flagged by the most effective careless responding indices slightly improves the reliability estimates of the measured scales. ([kupffer-et-al-2024-detecting-careless-responding-in-multidimensional-forced-choice-questionnaires.pdf])
- The manifestation of careless responding increases towards the end of the survey. ([kupffer-et-al-2024-detecting-careless-responding-in-multidimensional-forced-choice-questionnaires.pdf])
