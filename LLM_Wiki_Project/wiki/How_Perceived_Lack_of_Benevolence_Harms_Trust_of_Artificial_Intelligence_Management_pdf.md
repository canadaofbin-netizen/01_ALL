# How Perceived Lack of Benevolence Harms Trust of Artificial Intelligence Management.pdf

This article explores the impact of replacing human managers with AI on employee trust. Through a field study of delivery riders in China and three experiments, the authors demonstrate that AI managers are perceived as less benevolent than human managers because they lack the ability to genuinely experience emotions. This perceived lack of benevolence significantly reduces employees' trust in AI management. Furthermore, the research shows that this negative effect is stronger in work contexts that demand high empathy. The findings highlight the critical role of emotional capability and benevolence in human-to-AI interactions in the workplace.
