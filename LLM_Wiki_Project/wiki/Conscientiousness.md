# Concept

## Definition
Conscientiousness is one of the Big Five personality traits characterized by a tendency to be responsible, organized, hard-working, and goal-directed. It is widely considered the most robust and consistent personality predictor of job performance across various occupations.

## Key Details
- **Subdimensions**: Conscientiousness is generally broken down into two primary subdimensions:
  - *Orderliness*: A preference for maintaining structure, routines, and order.
  - *Industriousness*: The tendency to work efficiently, persistently, and purposefully.
- **The Traditional Consensus**: Traditionally, conscientious employees were expected to excel, and they thrived particularly well when using passive 20th-century technologies that required human-imposed structure.
- **The Modern Challenge**: Recent research in [[AI_and_Work]] by [[Pok_Man_Tang]] and colleagues (see [[Tang_Koopman_McClean_et_al_2022]]) challenges this consensus. They demonstrate that the orderliness subdimension creates a non-complementary mismatch with modern intelligent machines (which are themselves highly systematic). This mismatch increases role ambiguity and can actually reduce task performance for orderly employees.
