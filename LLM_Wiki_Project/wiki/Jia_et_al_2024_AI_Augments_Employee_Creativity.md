# Jia_et_al_2024_AI_Augments_Employee_Creativity

This article investigates how artificial intelligence (AI) affects employee creativity through a sequential division of labor. Through a field experiment at a telemarketing firm, the authors demonstrate that assigning repetitive lead-generation tasks to AI increases human employees' creativity during subsequent sales persuasion, thereby boosting sales. Crucially, this AI-augmented creativity is skill-biased, primarily benefiting higher-skilled employees. Qualitative interviews further reveal that this job design change fosters positive emotions and innovative problem-solving in experts, while often causing stress and demoralization among lower-skilled workers.
