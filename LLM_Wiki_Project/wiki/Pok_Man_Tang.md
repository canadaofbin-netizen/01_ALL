# Entity

## Background
Pok Man Tang is a researcher affiliated with the Department of Management at the University of Georgia. He is a prominent author in the field of organizational behavior and technology.

## Key Attributes
His research focuses on:
- Human-AI interaction in the workplace
- Social affiliation processes and attachment theory
- The role of personality traits like [[Conscientiousness]] in navigating modern technology

He is a lead author on studies investigating how intelligent machines impact employee well-being, social behavior, and performance, such as the widely cited [[Tang_Koopman_McClean_et_al_2022]] paper exploring AI through the lens of [[Complementarity_Theory]] and [[Role_Theory]].
