# Slow drift rate predicts ADHD symptomology over and above executive dysfunction.pdf

This paper investigates whether slow drift rate, an index of evidence accumulation speed derived from the diffusion model, better predicts parent and teacher ratings of ADHD than standard metrics of executive functioning (EF). Using structural equation modeling in a sample of 201 children, the study found that while poor EF and slow drift rate each independently predicted higher general ADHD symptomology, only drift rate continued to significantly predict symptoms when both were included in the model. The authors conclude that drift rate is a superior, mechanistically specific cognitive predictor of ADHD compared to traditional EF measures.
