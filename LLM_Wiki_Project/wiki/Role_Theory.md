# Concept

## Definition
Role Theory (Kahn et al., 1964; Rizzo et al., 1970) examines how individuals in organizations perceive their roles and the expectations placed upon them by their environment and role senders. 

## Key Details
Two critical role perceptions within organizational psychology include:
- **Role Breadth Self-Efficacy**: The employee's perceived confidence in their ability to take on broader, more proactive duties beyond standard job requirements (Parker, 1998).
- **Role Ambiguity**: The degree of uncertainty an employee feels regarding what is expected of them in their role.

Recent research on [[AI_and_Work]] by [[Pok_Man_Tang]] et al. (see [[Tang_Koopman_McClean_et_al_2022]]) demonstrates that interacting with intelligent machines affects both of these role perceptions. Specifically, the introduction of AI and algorithms can increase role breadth self-efficacy for some, but can also induce role ambiguity—particularly for employees high in [[Conscientiousness]] (specifically orderliness), which negatively impacts their performance.
