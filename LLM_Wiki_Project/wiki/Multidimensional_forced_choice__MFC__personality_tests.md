# Multidimensional forced choice (MFC) personality tests

Assessments and questionnaire response formats used in industrial and organizational psychology to measure traits while minimizing response biases and resisting faking. Participants rank or choose among multiple items/statements representing different constructs (often in blocks like triplets) according to how well the items describe them, rather than rating items individually.
