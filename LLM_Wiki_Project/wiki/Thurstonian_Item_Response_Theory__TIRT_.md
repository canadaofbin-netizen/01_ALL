# Thurstonian Item Response Theory (TIRT)

A modern psychometric scoring method and mathematical model used to obtain normative scores from MFC (Multidimensional forced-choice) ranking data and questionnaires. It estimates normative trait levels based on Thurstone's law of comparative judgment.
